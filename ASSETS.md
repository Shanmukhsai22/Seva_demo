# Devotional artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved into the project at `public/images/devotional-hero.png`. It is decorative generated imagery, not a photograph of an identified temple. No real Ayyagaru likeness or supplied organisational logo was used.

Final prompt:

> Create a premium photographic website hero asset for Ekatvam Seva, a Telugu Hindu spiritual community website. Wide landscape image, approximately 3:2 composition. A beautifully aged brass deepam oil lamp with a real tiny warm flame in the foreground right, orange marigold flowers and a few ivory jasmine flowers at its base, ornate South Indian temple stone arch receding behind it, softly out of focus inner sanctum and warm sun shafts, incense haze. Sacred, serene and authentic atmosphere, cinematic editorial photography, tactile brass and carved stone textures, warm amber highlights, deep brown shadows, muted cream and terracotta palette. Subject on right two thirds, dark unobtrusive left edge. No people, no letters, no words, no text, no symbols added on top, no watermark. This is a decorative atmospheric image, not a portrayal of a particular actual temple.

Interface ornaments are native SVG geometry. Icons use Lucide. Fonts are DM Sans, Playfair Display, Noto Sans Telugu, and Noto Sans Devanagari loaded through Google Fonts, with system fallbacks. Their requests can be self-hosted before deployment if desired.
